# 00–09 Person Options — Three Systems

## Encoding Keys

| Digit | Dominic | Major EN | Major HE |
|-------|---------|----------|----------|
| 0 | O | S / Z | ס |
| 1 | A | T / D | ל |
| 2 | B | N | ב |
| 3 | C | M | ג/ק/כ |
| 4 | D | R | ד |
| 5 | E | L | ח/כ |
| 6 | S | J / SH / CH | ש |
| 7 | G | K / hard-G | ר |
| 8 | H | F / V | מ |
| 9 | N | P / B | פ |

**Dominic** = person's initials (first-name initial → first digit, last-name initial → second digit)  
**Major EN** = first two major consonant sounds of the name  
**Major HE** = same rule, using the Hebrew phonetic map above
